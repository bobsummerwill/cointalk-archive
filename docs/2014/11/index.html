CryptoCard Secure Bitcoin Wallets!

November 17, 2014UncategorizedKyle Kurbegovich

Crypto cards are BIP38 laser etched offline wallets! They’re sleek, scratch resistant, water & flameproof up to 600c! Keep your Bitcoin and crypto-currencies safe with the most secure Cold Storage system. Easy and fast to use, these passphrase protected anodized aluminium offline wallets will last for over 75 years!
